Initial release of Tumblr Downloader:

• Download images and videos from Tumblr posts
• Automatic clipboard detection for Tumblr URLs
• Share from other apps to start downloading
• Built-in WebView login for private/restricted posts
• Persistent cookies across restarts
• Download queue with pause/resume/retry
• Download history with Room database
• Built-in media viewer
• Custom save directory via SAF
• Material Design 3 UI
• Multi-language support (EN/ZH)
• Network-aware auto-pause
• Adult content detection
• Fully open source (MIT)
