Tumblr Downloader is an Android app that lets you download images and videos from Tumblr posts.

Features:
• Download media from Tumblr posts with a single link
• Automatic clipboard detection for Tumblr URLs
• Share from other apps to start downloading
• Built-in WebView login to access private/restricted posts
• Persistent cookies for session reuse across restarts
• Download queue with pause/resume/retry support
• Download history with Room database persistence
• Built-in media viewer for images and videos
• Custom save directory via Storage Access Framework
• Multi-language support (English and Chinese)
• Material Design 3 UI with dark mode support
• Network-aware: auto-pauses on connection loss
• Adult content detection and filtering

Privacy:
• Login credentials are stored locally using Android Keystore
• No analytics or tracking
• No ads
• Fully open source under MIT license

Requires Android 11 or later.
